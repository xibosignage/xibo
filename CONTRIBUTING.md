# Contributing

## How can I contribute?
To find out how to contribute to Xibo, take a look at our Documentation: https://docs.xibosignage.com/developer/contributing-to-xibo.


## Integrate or extend
Xibo has extensive options for integration and extension, whether that be using the API to connect with 3rd parties or
making exciting new widgets. Find out more in our
[developer documentation](https://docs.xibosignage.com/developer).
